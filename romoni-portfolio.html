less friction.

Salon at Home: rethinking beauty service discovery and booking to create a calmer, clearer, more trustworthy experience.

Project
Salon at Home
Category
Lifestyle / Wellness app
Tools
Adobe XD
Scope
Conceptual UI/UX redesign
View on Dribbble ↗

The challenge

Users faced difficulties in discovering services, trusting beauticians, and navigating a cluttered interface. The original design lacked transparency about safety protocols and failed to highlight service professionals effectively.

The goal: Enhance service discovery, build trust with beauticians, and streamline the booking process for a calm, user-friendly experience.

Three screens. A clearer journey.

The redesign focused on three core screens to improve usability and trust:

  • Home screen: Showcased services and reassured users with safety and trust indicators.
  • Services screen: Simplified booking with intuitive quantity updates and clear pricing.
  • Beautician profile: Introduced professional bios and credentials to build credibility.

Starting with real needs

Home / start screen

  • As a new user, I want to easily browse available services.
  • As a safety-conscious customer, I want reassurance about hygiene practices.
  • As an interested user, I want to view beautician profiles directly.

Services list

  • As a browsing user, I want to see all facial options with pricing and duration.
  • As a busy professional, I want to update service quantities quickly.
  • As a multitasker, I want easy navigation with tabs.

Beautician profile

  • As a first-time user, I want to view experience and verification badges.
  • As a returning customer, I want the option to rebook the same beautician.

Less guessing. More clarity.

Key UX challenges addressed
BeforeAfter
Deep category treesFlat scrollable tiles with icons and tabs
No beautician detailsFull beautician profile cards
Lack of safety informationDedicated safety assurance section
No quantity feedbackLive counters for service selection

A softer visual language

  • Color palette: Clean whites and soft pinks to create a calm, inviting feel.
  • Typography: Rounded sans-serif fonts for a modern, approachable tone.
  • Layout: Modular card-based design with clear calls to action and logical flow.

The intended impact

This was a conceptual redesign, so these are anticipated outcomes rather than measured results:

  • Improved user trust through transparency and personalization.
  • A smoother, faster service booking journey.
  • Higher conversion rates from first-time users.

What I took away

Designing for lifestyle services demands empathy, clarity, and simplicity. Users need to feel safe, informed, and in control. Building trust through storytelling and visual design is equally important as ensuring smooth functionality.
